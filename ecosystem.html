PAGE 14 — THE CAPABILITY ECOSYSTEM

No Important Capability Is Built Alone

BTCC connects capability rather than replacing existing institutions. We build a network across multiple dimensions of the technology ecosystem to turn research into national impact.

INSTITUTIONAL ARCHITECTURE

Ecosystem Dimensions

PARTNERSHIP 01

Research Partners

Universities, national laboratories, and specialized research centres generating frontier scientific insights and foundational intellectual property.

PARTNERSHIP 02

Technology Partners

Deep-tech companies, innovators, and technology developers advancing prototype validation, system integration, and hardware-software architectures.

PARTNERSHIP 03

Industry Partners

Enterprises, manufacturers, supply-chain aggregators, and MSMEs with capacity for precision fabrication, scaling, and commercialization.

PARTNERSHIP 04

Mission Partners

Institutions, consortia, and strategic bodies contributing dedicated operational resources to defined national capability missions.

PARTNERSHIP 05

Capital Partners

Patient capital investors, development finance institutions, strategic venture funds, and mission funders aligned on long capability cycles.

PARTNERSHIP 06

Public System Partners

Ministries, departments, PSUs, regulators, and public institutions collaborating where appropriate on deployment pathways and public testbeds.

BTCC Fellows & Expert Network
