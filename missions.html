PAGE 12 — CAPABILITY MISSIONS

From Identified Gaps to Coordinated Action

BTCC develops and facilitates mission-oriented programmes around defined technology and capability requirements. These represent founding hypotheses to bridge research and deployment.

Mission Architecture

A BTCC mission brings together: Research Institutions + Technology Developers + Industry + Talent + Capital + Public Systems to pursue measurable capability outcomes.

FOUNDING MISSION 01

Autonomous Systems for Bharat

Connecting AI, robotics, drones, sensors, communications and related technologies towards practical capability development.

Focus: Unmanned & Autonomous Systems
Pathway: Civilian & Strategic Demonstration
Ecosystem: Laboratories, Industry & MSMEs
Outcome: Deployable Native Platforms
Partner on this Mission
FOUNDING MISSION 02

Cyber Resilience for Bharat

Strengthening resilience across digital enterprises, public systems, healthcare, industry and emerging technology environments.

Focus: Digital Security & Critical Infra
Pathway: Real-time Testing & Hardening
Ecosystem: Enterprise, Academia & CERTs
Outcome: Sovereign Security Architecture
Partner on this Mission
FOUNDING MISSION 03

Dual-Use Technology Translation

Creating pathways between civilian research, strategic applications, testing, industry, capital and deployment.

Focus: Civilian & Strategic Convergence
Pathway: Rapid Prototyping & Testing
Ecosystem: R&D Labs, Capital & Industry
Outcome: Scalable Domestic Manufacturing
Partner on this Mission
