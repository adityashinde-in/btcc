PAGE 13 — BTCC KNOWLEDGE

Evidence Before Intervention

BTCC develops and supports research, assessments, methodologies, datasets, technology intelligence and knowledge products to improve understanding of emerging capabilities. Evidence that travels beyond the programme — knowledge must become institutional capability.

FLAGSHIP INTELLIGENCE ASSETS

The BTCC Knowledge Architecture

INDEX

BTCC Technology Capability Index

An annual multi-dimensional assessment evaluating national technology capability across critical sectors, research density, supply-chain autonomy, and deployment readiness.

OUTLOOK

BTCC Technology Capability Outlook

Forward-looking strategic analysis mapping technological horizons over 5, 10, and 20-year cycles to inform national policy and institutional investment.

REGISTER

BTCC Capability Gap Register

A structured institutional registry of identified technology and supply-chain vulnerabilities, infrastructure deficits, and translation bottlenecks requiring coordinated missions.

OBSERVATORY

BTCC Technology Readiness Observatory

Continuous tracking of technology readiness levels (TRL) and deployment readiness levels (DRL) across domestic laboratories and deep-tech enterprises.

BRIEFS

BTCC Technology Briefs

Concise, evidence-led briefs addressing targeted technology questions, policy standards, procurement pathways, and operational challenges.

DATA & FRAMEWORKS

Datasets & Methodologies

Open institutional datasets, assessment frameworks, benchmark methodologies, and mission documentation curated for public systems and research partners.
